# Financial Policy Decision Register

**Reviewed:** 2026-10-13  
**Status:** Open owner decisions — this file records decisions needed before changing accounting semantics. It is not approval to change production behavior.

| FIN-POL-001 | How should invoice-level discounts and taxes be allocated to returned lines? | Original line totals are stored before invoice-level discount/tax; returning a line can exceed the invoice's net amount if the header discount was material. | Returns against source invoices with a non-zero header discount/tax now fail closed before mutation. Return-level tax is also blocked; proportional net-value allocation is not implemented. | Define proportional allocation/snapshots for line net value and tax before re-enabling adjusted-invoice returns; add full and partial return tests, including already-paid invoices. | SAFETY GUARD IMPLEMENTED — ALLOCATION POLICY NEEDS OWNER DECISION |
| FIN-POL-002 | What happens when a sales return references an invoice that was fully or partially paid? | Reducing AR by the return amount can produce a negative invoice balance; that may represent a customer credit/refund but requires an explicit accounting treatment. | Sales return now fails closed if its total exceeds the source invoice's remaining receivable after prior returns. Fully paid invoices therefore cannot be returned through the current workflow until credit/refund policy exists. | Choose refund, customer credit, or AR-only correction and the corresponding ledger accounts/workflow; then enable returns above remaining AR with tests. | SAFETY GUARD IMPLEMENTED — CREDIT/REFUND POLICY NEEDS OWNER DECISION |
| FIN-POL-003 | What happens when a purchase return references an invoice already paid to the supplier? | Reducing supplier outstanding by return total can make the supplier balance negative; this may be a supplier credit/refund but should not be implicit. | Purchase return now fails closed if its total exceeds the source invoice's remaining payable after prior returns. Supplier payment allocation also subtracts posted purchase returns before calculating the remaining payable. | FIN-POL-004 | How should trip stock variance be posted after physical closing count? | Settlement records expected/actual quantities and variance value, but the stock ledger adjustment and approval flow need an explicit policy. | `settleTrip()` writes settlement lines/variance and closes the trip; this flow does not itself create a stock adjustment movement for the variance. | Decide whether settlement auto-posts an adjustment or requires a separately approved adjustment; define valuation and audit reference. | NEEDS OWNER DECISION |
| FIN-POL-005 | How should trip cash over/short be posted and approved? | A non-zero cash variance is currently recorded but not posted to an over/short ledger account. | `settleTrip()` calculates and stores `cash_variance`, then completes the trip. | Select cash over/short accounts, approval thresholds, and whether variance can be posted after closure. | NEEDS OWNER DECISION |
| FIN-POL-006 | Are unallocated customer receipts/supplier payments allowed as party advances? | Payment amount may exceed allocations to documents; the ledger and party summary treat the full payment as a party-balance movement. | Allocations cannot exceed payment amount or document due, but a remainder can stay unallocated. | Confirm advance/credit semantics, how the balance is displayed, and how future invoices consume it. | NEEDS OWNER DECISION |
| FIN-POL-007 | How are payment/account/invoice currency mismatches handled? | Posting currently stores currency fields and updates balances without an exchange-rate workflow. | No exchange-rate conversion or realized FX gain/loss path was verified in this audit. | Confirm single-currency-per-tenant rules or specify rate source, rate snapshot, rounding and FX ledger treatment. | NEEDS OWNER DECISION |
| FIN-POL-008 | How should header discounts and taxes be posted and valued across sales, purchases and returns? | Purchase header discount/tax can make inventory/AP ledger totals differ; tax on sales/returns is included in revenue/return totals instead of a dedicated tax account. Purchase returns have the same gross-cost vs net-total mismatch. | Fail-closed guard added: any non-zero tax is rejected on sales, purchases and returns; non-zero header discounts are rejected on purchases and purchase returns. Sales and sales-return discounts remain supported as net revenue/return reductions. No discount/tax accounting entries or line allocation are inferred. | Decide tax liability/input-tax accounts and treatment, whether discounts reduce inventory valuation, and proportional line/return allocation with rounding. Then implement postings and source-document ledger reconciliation tests before re-enabling blocked fields. | SAFETY GUARD IMPLEMENTED — ACCOUNTING POLICY NEEDS OWNER DECISION |
| FIN-POL-009 | Is multi-currency posting allowed before FX accounting is implemented? | There is no exchange-rate conversion or realized/unrealized FX workflow. A document in a different currency cannot be safely posted against base-currency ledger amounts. | Implemented a fail-closed single-currency guard: sales, purchases, returns and payments must use the organization's `default_currency`; payment/expense financial accounts must also match that base currency. Focused regression tests prove rejected posts do not mutate business data. | Multi-currency remains disabled. Before enabling it, approve the FX model: rate source, transaction-date rate snapshot, rounding, account currency semantics, and realized/unrealized FX ledger treatment. | SAFETY GUARD IMPLEMENTED — FX SUPPORT NEEDS OWNER DECISION |
| FIN-POL-010 | How should retrospective supplier volume rebates (for example, 5% after a period) be booked and settled? | The commercial benefit may be a supplier credit note, a reduction of future payables, a receivable, or a bank/cash refund. Treating it as cash or silently reducing AP without an approved rule would misstate balances. | NEXORA now records an auditable accrued rebate claim against posted purchase invoices in a selected date range. The base is the invoice total less posted returns recorded at claim time; invoice use is unique and currency is kept separate. No cash, supplier-payable, or GL posting is made automatically. | Owner/accountant must choose the legal document and accounting route, eligible invoice dates, return/credit treatment, approval and settlement workflow. Only then implement the approved ledger/AP posting with reconciliation tests. | TRACKING IMPLEMENTED — ACCOUNTING/SETTLEMENT NEEDS OWNER DECISION |
